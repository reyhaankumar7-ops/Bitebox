Pizza

Pizza is a savory dish of Italian origin made with a flattened wheat dough base, typically topped with tomatoes, cheese, and baked at a high temperature.



Burger

A burger (hamburger) is a popular sandwich made with a cooked patty—traditionally beef, chicken, or a vegetarian alternative like aloo tikki or paneer—placed inside a sliced bun and topped with ingredients like lettuce, onions, tomatoes, cheese, and sauces.



Fries

French fries (or chips) are pieces of potato that have been deep-fried in fat until crisp on the outside and soft on the inside.



Spaghetti

Spaghetti is a long, thin, solid, cylindrical pasta made from milled wheat—traditionally durum-wheat semolina—and water.
